## IRC Status Prefixes

The `@` and `+` symbols are not part of the historical nickname.

They are stored separately in the `irc_status_prefixes` column:

- `@` = operator status marker in the captured listing
- `+` = voice status marker given by operators in the captured listing
- `+@` = combined captured prefix sequence, normalized separately from the display nickname

The cleaned historical identifier is stored as `display_nickname`.

## Historical Rotation Note

This 2002-11-28 batch is a snapshot, not a complete census of every Canal Barra participant across all years.

Some older operator nicknames documented in the June 1999 operators meeting — including JU-RJ, ClaraM and Daiane-de-Ipanema — do not appear in this 2002 list. That absence is historically meaningful: it documents turnover, access-cycle change and migration of some participants to other channels or social spaces.

## Privacy

This batch preserves historical nicknames only. It does not include civil names, emails, IP addresses, full hostmasks, user photos or private messages.
