# Film Media Boundary

## Purpose

This directory is reserved for future public film, press and storytelling material related to Canal Barra, including trailers, reels, press notes, interviews, production notes and release metadata.

## Evidence boundary

Film and storytelling materials are not independent historical evidence.

They may be used as:

- cultural impact material;
- public dissemination material;
- narrative adaptation context;
- press and media trail for the archive.

They must not be used as:

- external proof of the repository thesis;
- independent verification of founding dates;
- independent verification of participant identity;
- proof that Canal Barra was the first social network in Brazil.

## Safe formulation

```text
The film and media materials are derived interpretive layers based on the Canal Barra archive. They may document the cultural afterlife of the archive, but they do not replace academic, archival, dataset or participant evidence.
```
