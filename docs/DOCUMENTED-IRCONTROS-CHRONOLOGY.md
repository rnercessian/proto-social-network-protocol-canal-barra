# Chronological Index of Documented IRContros — Canal Barra

> **Scope:** This research index includes in-person IRC-associated gatherings and cultural events publicly linked to Canal Barra. Each event has a distinct classification and evidence status. It is **not an exhaustive record** of all encounters between 1997 and 2007.
>
> **Historical dates are not Wayback capture dates.** The date a photograph was published, a caption was catalogued, or a page was captured cannot automatically be treated as the day an event occurred. Unconfirmed dates remain explicitly marked.

## Equal status as chronological entries

The performances on **October 15 and October 29, 2000** each have an independent event identifier, date, provenance and evidence status, at the **same level of chronological documentation** as the 2002 Fifth Anniversary Party. Equal chronological treatment does **not** imply equal crowd size, cultural importance or verified attendance by the IRC community.

## Privacy and source limitations

This index excludes private audience identities, personal contact information, email addresses, payment details, banking information and information explicitly shared confidentially. Do not infer the composition of an audience, a person's identity from an image caption, or how many IRC participants attended.
