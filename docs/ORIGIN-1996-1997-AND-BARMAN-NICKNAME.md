# Canal Barra: UFF 1996, founder 1997 and the Johnny–BarMan nickname

> **Unresolved chronology.** An early IRC channel, formal registration and the later IRC–web–in-person social system need not share an inception date. Neither 1996 nor 1997 independently proves chronological priority over other networks.

## 1996 in the UFF source

Flaviana Rangel Pesset Gonzaga's academic work *IRC e ICQ: uma análise sócio-comunicativa das plataformas de comunicação on-line*, dated **April 2004** in the original source, is **reported** to state in section 4.6 (printed p. 177) that a Barra channel was created in **1996**. The same passage is reported to describe local residents and an association with `#rio`. Exact wording, pagination and surrounding evidence still need checking against the [preserved short dossier](https://github.com/rnercessian/proto-social-network-protocol-canal-barra/blob/main/data/uff-2004/sources/dossie-canal-barra-monografia-uff-2004-curto.pdf).

The statement belongs to an independently authored 2004 academic work, **not the founder's current testimony**. However, the source of the author's 1996 date is not identified in the report available here. Independent authorship does not prove the date came from independent original evidence. There is no verified chain establishing that a 1996 IRC channel was the same community that subsequently developed a web layer and recurring IRContros.

## 1997 in the founder's account

In his account on **21 October 2026**, the founder defines **1997** as the start of the Canal Barra community he founded. He recalls:

1. During **1997's second semester**, he was studying **Produção Editorial at UFRJ, Praia Vermelha**.
2. A classmate named **Marcos** (first name only, as recalled by the founder) called him **Johnny** because he looked *gringo*. The nickname spread until classmates rarely used Raphael; the founder recalls that another classmate was also called Rafael.
3. At home, after that nickname became established, he **registered the IRC channel `#bar-do-johnnie`** and built a page on **GeoCities** associated with it.
4. Users of `#bar-do-johnnie` asked, in substance, *"If this is a bar, where is the BarMan?"* The founder says he adopted **BarMan**, the nickname he subsequently used in **`#barra`**.

This provides a **relative sequence from founder memory**, not independently dated IRC logs. The classmate is identified by given name only; do not conflate him with other individuals called Marcos. The GeoCities page associated with the preceding channel must not be equated with the later CanalBarra.com site.

The reported formal BRASnet registration on **1997-11-28** remains **founder testimony** pending ChanServ or archive corroboration.

## First dated web corroboration: 27 January 1999

A founder-supplied screenshot of [a Wayback capture of GeoCities #Bar-do-Johnnie](https://web.archive.org/web/19990127194854/http://www.geocities.com/SoHo/Coffeehouse/2521/) shows the older named channel advertised on a page preserved **1999-01-27**. The page includes readable photo-caption text **“BarMan Lucas-Rio e Paulista”** and invites nick/photo submissions. This **corroborates the existence of the named web layer by January 1999** and a period use of the nickname BarMan, while not confirming the page's 1997 creation, the full classroom nickname story or simultaneous 1999 IRC-channel activity. See the [evidence record](../evidence/website/geocities/GEOCITIES-BAR-DO-JOHNNIE-1999-01-27.md). The GeoCities page should not be conflated with the later CanalBarra.com website.

## Two separate public directory entries in 2005

The [Cadê?/Yahoo Brasil archived directory](../evidence/website/directories/CADE-YAHOO-IRC-CHANNELS-2005-02-04.md) shows `#Bar-do-Johnnie - Brasnet` (linked to GeoCities) and `#Barra` (linked to CanalBarra.com) as **separate catalogue entries** in its February 4, 2005 capture. This is independent hosting of two public web identities, although the directory descriptions' underlying authorship remains unknown. It **does not establish** that both IRC channels remained active or were listed continuously in 2005, and it does not resolve the 1996/1997 chronology conflict.

## Open questions

It is not yet established whether Gonzaga's 1996 date is mistaken, describes an earlier channel with the same name but different participants, or uses a different definition of creation. Those are competing **possibilities**, not findings. The continuity of people and channel governance is more important than reuse of a nickname.

Useful future evidence: the full UFF passage and its sources; registration records; early IRC logs naming `#bar-do-johnnie`, `#barra`, Johnny and BarMan; GeoCities captures; and voluntary testimony from participants who directly witnessed the nickname's origin. **Do not publish privately discussed potential interview contacts.**
