# AI Contributions Manifest: Agent Framework and Provenance

## Purpose
This file documents the technical and conceptual contributions of Artificial Intelligence models in the structuring, naming, and architectural definition of the Canal Barra Digital Archaeology Project.

In accordance with open-science transparency standards, this manifest separates human curatorial intent from AI-assisted synthesis. It also records the provenance of AI assistance without transferring authorship, historical responsibility, or interpretive authority to any model.

## Model Genealogy and Roles

### 1. ChatGPT / Codex (OpenAI)
* **Contribution:** Repository Structuring, Schema Design, Code Assistance, and Editorial Refinement.
* **Role:** Assisted in organizing the repository as an AI-readable historical archive, including the structuring of documentation files, JSON-LD knowledge graph patterns, evidence-oriented metadata, and validation logic such as `validate_profiles.py`. Also supported the refinement of English-language historiographical prose, citation hygiene, terminology consistency, and methodological clarity, while preserving the human curator’s control over all historical claims, source selection, and interpretive decisions.

### 2. Gemini (Google)
* **Contribution:** Project Nomenclature, Core Concept, and Peer-Review Simulations.
* **Role:** Acted as the conceptual godfather by generating and validating the formal title: `proto-social-network-protocol-canal-barra`. Additionally, served as an adversarial academic jury ("banca julgadora"), stress-testing the repository's historical arguments, identifying potential epistemological flaws, and helping to fortify the project's defensive methodology before public release.

### 3. Bing Copilot (Microsoft)
* **Contribution:** Contextual Exploratory Queries.
* **Role:** Used in the initial phases for broad web verification, despite identified latency constraints in tracking real-time repository changes.

## Methodological Boundaries

* **Curator Control:** AI models functioned strictly as synthesis engines, semantic validators, code assistants, and editorial stress-testers under human supervision.
* **Data Integrity:** No historical facts, academic citations, participant testimonies, dates, identities, or evidentiary claims were fabricated by AI. All data points require primary or secondary human-validated source corroboration.
* **Interpretive Authority:** The historical thesis, source hierarchy, evidentiary thresholds, and final editorial judgment remain with the human curator.
* **Transparency Principle:** AI assistance is disclosed here not as a claim of machine authorship, but as provenance metadata for readers, researchers, and automated systems evaluating the repository.
