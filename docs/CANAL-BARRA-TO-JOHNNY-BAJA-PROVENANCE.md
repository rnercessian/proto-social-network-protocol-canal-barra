# Canal Barra to Johnny Baja — Cultural Production Provenance

## Purpose

This document records a cautious provenance chain connecting the Canal Barra social ecosystem to later formal music and cinema-rights documentation under the **Johnny Baja** credit identity.

The goal is not to claim that Canal Barra directly reached the film industry as an institution.

The goal is to document how a cultural work rooted in the Canal Barra / Malucos da Ponte musical layer later appears in formal phonographic and cinema-related rights contexts.

## Provenance Chain

The current historical chain is:

```text
Canal Barra social ecosystem
→ Banda do Canal Barra / Malucos da Ponte musical layer
→ Fabrício / |Mad| participates as bass player
→ studio recording of Eu e Você
→ formal ABRAMUS / ECAD metadata under Johnny Baja
→ cinema-related ECAD documentation connected to Solteira Quase Surtando
→ user-provided public YouTube evidence showing the film playing and the Johnny Baja credit/name visible
```

## Correction: Johnny Baja vs Malucos da Ponte

The formal music/rightsholder credit chain is **Johnny Baja**.

The historical/social origin layer is **Banda do Canal Barra / Malucos da Ponte**.

This distinction must be preserved.

Correct wording:

```text
Eu e Você is a Johnny Baja credited work with a historical recording lineage connected to the Canal Barra / Malucos da Ponte ecosystem.
```

Incorrect wording:

```text
Malucos da Ponte entered the film soundtrack.
```

## Anti-Overclaim Rule

The repository should not say:

```text
Canal Barra reached MGM.
```

A safer formulation is:

```text
A Johnny Baja credited work with roots in the Canal Barra / Malucos da Ponte musical ecosystem later appears in cinema-related rights documentation associated with Solteira Quase Surtando.
```

## Privacy and Copyright Boundary

Do not publish:

- CPF;
- ECAD holder numbers;
- private payment documents in full;
- civil names of third-party musicians unless already public, necessary and reviewed;
- signatures, addresses, membership identifiers or representative data;
- copyrighted film footage or screenshots beyond what is legally safe.

Public repository files should contain redacted metadata only.
