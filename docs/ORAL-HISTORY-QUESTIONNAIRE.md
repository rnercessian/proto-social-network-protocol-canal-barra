# Oral History Questionnaire

## Purpose

This questionnaire is for collecting Canal Barra memories from former participants without exposing private data or forcing personal accusations.

The goal is to document the human layer: trust, status, friendships, events, help networks, rituals, conflicts and the transition between online nickname and real-life presence.

## Submission Rule

Contributors may answer using nickname-level identity only.

Do not include private addresses, phone numbers, documents, private messages, IP addresses, hostmasks or sensitive personal details.

## Basic Identification

```text
Historical nickname(s):
Approximate years active in #barra:
Main role, if any: user / voiced user / operator / master / webmaster / event organizer / musician / other
Do you authorize nickname-level citation? yes/no
Do you authorize civil-name citation? yes/no
```

## First Contact

```text
How did you first discover #barra?
Was CanalBarra.com part of your first contact with the community?
Did you enter through webchat, mIRC, another IRC client or recommendation from someone?
Was the website visible in the topic, entry message or through user promotion?
```

## Nickname and Reputation

```text
How important was your nickname to your identity in the channel?
Did people recognize you mainly by nickname, voice, photos, IRContros or reputation?
What made a nickname become respected?
What made a nickname lose credibility?
```

## Operators and Hierarchy

```text
How did people become trusted enough to receive + voice or @ operator status?
Was there informal mentorship from older members?
Were operators seen as protectors, authorities, friends, rivals or something else?
Do you remember cases where technical power did not match social legitimacy?
```

## Conflict and Moderation

```text
How were conflicts usually handled before kick or ban?
Did operators prefer dialogue, PVT warnings, public correction or immediate punishment?
Do you remember bot attacks, floods, impersonation, fights or abuse of power?
What counted as unacceptable behavior in #barra?
```

## IRContros

```text
Which IRContros did you attend?
What changed when people met physically after knowing each other by nickname?
Were there people who became more respected after appearing in person?
Were there people whose online persona changed after IRContros?
Did photos and captions on websites matter socially?
```

## Human Impact

```text
Did friendships, relationships, bands, jobs, projects or support networks come from #barra?
Do you know of marriages, long relationships, work opportunities or artistic projects connected to the channel?
Did the community help anyone during difficult moments?
```

## Website Role

```text
How often did you access CanalBarra.com?
Was it a homepage or frequent destination for you?
Did you use cadastros, photos, voting, events, rules or operator pages?
Did the website affect status, visibility or social circulation inside the channel?
```

## Final Memory

```text
What is the strongest image you still remember from #barra?
What should historians of the Brazilian internet understand about Canal Barra?
What would be false or unfair if future readers reduced Canal Barra to just a chat room?
```

## Core Sentence

**The oral history layer should document how Canal Barra transformed nicknames into social presence, trust, memory and real-world consequences.**
