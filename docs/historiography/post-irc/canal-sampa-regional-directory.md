# Post-IRC Continuity: Canal Sampa Regional Directory

This record belongs to post-IRC Canal Barra continuity and is not used as direct evidence for the 1996–2007 Canal Barra IRC/BRASnet social phenomenon. Canal Sampa was a later regional portal associated with the Canal Barra organization, preserved in the Internet Archive in 2011 at `canalsampa.com.br`; the archived page explicitly presents it as "o novo portal da Organização Canal Barra Consultoria em Sistemas".

The evidence value is limited and specific: Canal Sampa indicates that, after the original IRC period, Canal Barra attempted to replicate its post-IRC portal/directory model in another regional market, extending the brand and directory logic beyond Rio de Janeiro. Preserved source: https://web.archive.org/web/20111010100310/http://canalsampa.com.br/
