# Mad / Fabrício — Historical Note

## Purpose

This document records the historical role and public legacy traces of **Fabrício**, known in the Canal Barra ecosystem by the nickname **|Mad|**.

The goal is to preserve his relevance to the Canal Barra and Malucos da Ponte history without turning the repository into a private biographical dossier.

## Historical Role

Founder statement and existing repository records connect **|Mad|** to multiple layers of the Canal Barra ecosystem:

- recurring historical nickname identity;
- operator / access-list context;
- IRContro and event-photo captions;
- Banda do Canal Barra / Malucos da Ponte cultural layer;
- bass performance and rehearsal memory;
- music-related legacy of the Canal Barra period.

## Studio Recordings

Founder statement: Mad recorded two songs in studio through channel-by-channel recording sessions:

- **Eu e Você**;
- **Só Por Querer**.

Founder statement further says **Eu e Você** later entered the soundtrack of **Solteira Quase Surtando**, a film released in 2020 and described by the founder as co-produced by Metro Goldwyn Mayer.

This soundtrack/film claim is important, but it needs external verification before being treated as confirmed public evidence in this repository.

## Rehearsal Recordings and Bass Contributions

Founder statement: beyond the studio recordings, Mad appears in numerous rehearsal recordings.

Founder statement also credits Mad with significant bass-line contributions in the Malucos da Ponte repertoire, including **Corpo Dourado**.

According to founder statement, **Corpo Dourado** was the encore song at the Canal Barra 5th anniversary party in 2002.
