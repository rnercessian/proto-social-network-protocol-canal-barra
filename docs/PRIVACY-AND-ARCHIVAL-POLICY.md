# Privacy and Archival Policy

## Purpose

This repository preserves historical evidence of Canal Barra while minimizing exposure of private individuals.

The project documents social structure, not civil identity.

## Nickname Policy

IRC nicknames are treated as historical pseudonymous identifiers of public/community presence, reputation and structure inside Canal Barra.

Nicknames may be used to document:

- presence in historical nickname lists;
- operator or access-level status;
- participation in public/community records;
- inclusion in VIP lists;
- aggregated community rankings or votes;
- appearance in archival screenshots where no sensitive data is exposed.

## Excluded Data

The public repository must not include:

- civil names linked to nicknames;
- photographs of users without current permission;
- private messages;
- email addresses;
- IP addresses;
- full hostmasks;
- telephone numbers;
- private addresses or private locations;
- sensitive personal information;
- allegations or intimate biographical claims about private individuals.

## Publication Purpose

The publication purpose is archival, historical and documentary.

The repository aims to preserve evidence of identity persistence, reputation, governance, physical gatherings, community memory and social structure in an IRC/BRASnet-based historical community.

## Data Minimization

Whenever possible, records should use the minimum amount of information needed to support the historical claim.

For example, a VIP list should preserve the event, date and nickname entries, but not civil identity or personal contact information.

A community vote should preferably be published in aggregated form unless the full voting record was already public, historically necessary and safe to preserve.

## Removal or Obfuscation Requests

A participant historically represented by a nickname may request removal or obfuscation of that nickname from public datasets.

Requests will be reviewed in good faith and processed when technically and legally appropriate.

## Archival Principle

**Nicknames document the social network. Civil identity remains protected.**
