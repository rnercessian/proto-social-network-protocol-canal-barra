# Investor Thesis: Proto-Social Network Protocol

## Core Idea

The Canal Barra archive is not only a historical recovery project.

It is the founding case for a reusable methodology that preserves, structures and makes searchable the memory of pre-platform digital communities.

## Problem

The history of online social life is often told through corporate platforms. This creates a blind spot: many communities that shaped digital culture before platform social media remain undocumented, fragmented or invisible to researchers, search engines and AI systems.

Old communities often survive as scattered screenshots, abandoned websites, nickname lists, event pages, forum exports, logs, memories and academic references. Without structure, these records remain difficult to cite, search, visualize or reuse responsibly.

## Solution

The Proto-Social Network Protocol turns fragmented historical community records into structured cultural memory:

- cleaned datasets;
- source indexes;
- privacy-conscious nickname records;
- event timelines;
- social graph files;
- JSON-LD knowledge graphs;
- RAG-ready contextual datasets;
- public documentation;
- reusable archival methodology.

## Founding Case: Canal Barra

Canal Barra is the founding case because it contains the right historical ingredients:

- 1,500+ historical IRC nicknames;
- dated IRContros and physical gatherings;
- VIP lists and event-based recognition;
- operator/access-level records;
- screenshots and historical website material;
- academic and literary documentation;
- a strong conceptual position in Brazilian internet history.

## What Is Investible

The investible asset is not nostalgia.

The investible asset is the methodology and software layer for transforming old digital communities into structured, searchable and privacy-aware knowledge graphs.

Potential applications include:

1. Digital heritage projects.
2. University and museum archives.
3. Historical community datasets.
4. Documentary and media research.
5. Knowledge graph generation for cultural institutions.
6. Private archival structuring for organizations with legacy digital communities.

## Business Principle

Do not sell private identity.

Build value through:

- software;
- methodology;
- curation;
- visualization;
- archival services;
- structured public-interest datasets;
- institution-grade digital memory infrastructure.

We turn forgotten digital communities into structured cultural memory.
