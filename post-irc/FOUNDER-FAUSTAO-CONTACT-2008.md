# Founder Contact with Domingão do Faustão — 2008

## Purpose

This document records a redacted historical contact between Raphael / BarMan and Globo regarding possible participation in **Domingão do Faustão**.

This record belongs to post-IRC Canal Barra continuity and is not used as direct evidence for the 1996–2007 Canal Barra IRC/BRASnet social phenomenon.

This is not direct evidence for Canal Barra as a social network.

It is relevant as later cultural and professional trajectory context connected to the founder's music ambitions after the Canal Barra / Malucos da Ponte period.

It also helps document a broader media-access context: many independent musicians and bands saw national television programs as an aspirational gate, while only a small fraction were selected or heard by major broadcast production systems.

## Source Context

Founder-provided email thread:

```text
Date of Globo reply: 2008-06-28
Original inquiry date: 2008-06-15
Subject: Domingão do Faustão - Outros
Sender of reply: Central Globo de Comunicação
```

The reply instructed Raphael to send CD and/or DVD material to the production team for selection review.

The founder states that the contact happened during his DBA period. The visible email thread should be treated carefully: the repository should not claim a DBA corporate email unless a header or source proving that specific sender address is added.

The founder also states that DBA Engenharia de Sistemas was a major outsourcing company at the time and that its role with Globo web properties reflected significant corporate trust. This should be treated as professional context pending external verification.

The original email included personal data such as address, phone number, email address and birth date. These details are intentionally excluded from this public repository.

## Historical Meaning

This source documents that the founder was still attempting to place his music in large Brazilian media contexts after the Canal Barra / Malucos da Ponte cycle.

It is not included as a success claim. It is included as evidence of aspiration, submission and gatekeeping around mainstream Brazilian music television.

It also supports a broader trajectory:

```text
Canal Barra / Malucos da Ponte musical layer
→ later Johnny Baja / founder music attempts
→ contact with Globo entertainment programming
→ later technical work in large media web environments
```
