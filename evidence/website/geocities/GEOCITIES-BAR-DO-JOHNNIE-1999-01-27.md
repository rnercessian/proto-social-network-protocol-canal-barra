## Why this changes the historical record

Before this snapshot was found, the founder's account of a GeoCities website attached to `#bar-do-johnnie` was indexed as retrospective testimony, without a known dated surviving URL. The **27 January 1999 Wayback capture** now supplies an independently archived temporal anchor that a GeoCities page publicly presented `#Bar-do-Johnnie` as a BRASnet conversation channel and advertised both chat access and user-related web content.

The **page caption itself** places the historical nicknames **`BarMan`**, **`Lucas-Rio`** and **`Paulista`** together in a labeled image block. This is much stronger than a 2026 recollection alone for those *nickname strings appearing together in a 1999 web document*. It is **not** visual identity verification: the associated photographs fail to render in the captured replay.

This archive is **not** the later domain `canalbarra.com`. It is a separate GeoCities address and explicitly advertises `#Bar-do-Johnnie`; its distinction from and temporal overlap with `#barra` are historical questions.

## Inferences and boundaries

**Supported by dated archived snapshot:** A page of `#Bar-do-Johnnie` was publicly preserved on 1999-01-27; the displayed page ties that name to BRASnet, chat access, nickname-labeled photo slots, invited nickname/photo submissions, ICQ references, and Barra da Tijuca context. It includes the nickname **BarMan** in a photo caption.

**Founder account corroborated in part:** The founder's claimed former `#bar-do-johnnie` GeoCities web layer is now supported as a **real preserved page by January 1999**, and his later nickname `BarMan` is documented on it. The date, authorship and entire 1997 genesis sequence remain testimony until earlier source evidence is found.

**Coexistence:** The founder dates formal `#barra` registration to 1997-11-28; this later 1999 `#bar-do-johnnie` page makes continued public presentation of its identity plausible after that registration. Without 1999 IRC logs or evidence the text was updated recently, **do not claim both channels were active at precisely the same time** or that they operated as one continuous user community.

**Relational efficacy:** The invitation to contribute a nickname/photo, photograph captions and voting navigation are historical **web interaction mechanisms**, not evidence of measured relationship quality, turnout, safety or efficacy.

**UFF 1996 conflict remains open:** This capture cannot confirm or refute Flaviana Gonzaga's reported 1996 creation date for a `#barra` channel. It proves neither that this `#barra` is the same as, nor different from, a possible 1996 `#barra`.
