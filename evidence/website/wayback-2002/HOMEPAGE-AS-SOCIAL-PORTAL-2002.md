# CanalBarra.com Homepage as Social Portal — 2002

## Source Context

A Wayback/homepage screenshot from **2002-11-28** shows the CanalBarra.com homepage functioning as a live social portal for the Canal Barra community.

The screenshot includes the event callout:

```text
28/11 - FESTA DE ANIVERSÁRIO DO CANAL BARRA (5 ANOS) NA ILHA DOS PESCADORES
```

It also shows that the homepage contained multiple community modules, not just static information.

## Observed Homepage Modules

The screenshot shows or indicates:

- Webchat entry to #Barra on BrasNet;
- event callout for the 5th anniversary party;
- event photo links;
- best-cadastro voting;
- cadastro promotion;
- recent cadastro listings;
- sponsor space;
- navigation to principal, cadastro, usuários, operadores, regras and Brasnet;
- visible count of online cadastros.
