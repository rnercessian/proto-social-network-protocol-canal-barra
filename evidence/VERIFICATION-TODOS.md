## Status vocabulary

Use precise status labels:

- `archived_snapshot`
- `author_recollection`
- `peer_recollection`
- `unverified`
- `unrecalled`
- `participant_denied`
- `declined_to_comment`
- `corroborated_by_peer`
- `restricted_visual_record`
- `abstract_authorized`
- `internal_reference_only`

Do not use `declined_to_comment` when a participant explicitly denies something. A denial is different from a refusal to answer.

## Form language rule

The repository is technical and in English. Participant forms may be in Portuguese and should use low-pressure language:

- "Você lembra...?"
- "Se não lembrar, tudo bem."
- "Quer corrigir algo?"

Avoid legalistic or intimidating wording such as testimony, witness, deposition, legal proof, validation or formal confirmation when talking to historical participants.
