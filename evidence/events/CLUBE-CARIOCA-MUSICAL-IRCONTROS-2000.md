# Cultural IRContros — Banda do Canal Barra at Clube Carioca (October 2000)

> **Classification:** Two distinct in-person music events, indexed as cultural IRContros associated with Canal Barra. This classification does **not** establish that an IRC-members' meeting took place or that IRC users formed the audience.
>
> **Contemporary public source:** [CanalBarra.com/banda — Wayback capture of March 3, 2001](https://web.archive.org/web/20010303094733/http://www.canalbarra.com/banda/), timestamp `20010303094733`.
>
> **Material examined:** Screenshot of the Wayback replay supplied by the founder on October 21, 2026; PNG 1920 × 1497 pixels, 388,066 bytes, SHA-256 `e4b49c3f4201f114db2fcfa94ee3fdc79dfca0b159c91205fed26853482eae50`. The screenshot binary has **not** been committed to the repository. The public Wayback URL is the reference.
>
> **Dating boundary:** The period web page announces October 29 and recalls a first festival performance on October 15, but it does **not print the year** next to those references. The founder explicitly identifies both events as occurring in **2000**. October 15 and 29, 2000 were both **Sundays**. Wayback captured the page on **March 3, 2001**, after both performances; this is not the page's original publication date.
>
> **Initial publication:** The founder remembers publishing the announcement less than a month before the October 29 event. No independently timestamped copy of that original version has yet been recovered.
>
> **Privacy:** Do not reproduce or index bank details, telephone numbers, email addresses or identifying financial instructions visible in the original page. Do not publish separately disclosed private remarks about audience composition.

## Event 1 — Sunday, October 15, 2000: first festival performance

- **Event ID:** `cb_ircontro_banda_clube_carioca_2000_10_15`.
- **Name:** Banda do Canal Barra — first appearance at the Clube Carioca Band Festival.
- **Historical date:** **Sunday, October 15, 2000**. Day and month are confirmed by a retrospective statement on the contemporary page; year is from the founder.
- **Venue context:** **Clube Carioca**, Rua Jardim Botânico, 650, Jardim Botânico neighborhood, Rio de Janeiro, RJ, Brazil. The address appears in the advertisement for the same festival, but no independently preserved first-night venue notice has been found.
- **Evidence:** The advertisement for the band's second show thanks friends and family who attended its **first festival performance on October 15**. This is a period textual statement that an earlier performance took place; it is not an attendee list.
- **Songs actually performed:** Not verified. The eight-song announcement for the subsequent October 29 show must not be assumed to be the October 15 setlist.
- **Audience size, ticket count and competition outcome:** Unknown. The founder's approximate crowd size and reported elimination **refer to October 29**, not this first appearance.
- **Historical significance:** A separately recorded public musical activity using the name **Banda do Canal Barra**; the cultural IRContro classification does not imply participation by the wider IRC community.

## Event 2 — Sunday, October 29, 2000: second performance and elimination round

- **Event ID:** `cb_ircontro_banda_clube_carioca_2000_10_29`.
- **Historical date:** **Sunday, October 29, 2000**, as identified by the founder; the original announcement supplies the day and month.
- **Venue:** **Clube Carioca**, Rua Jardim Botânico, 650, Jardim Botânico, Rio de Janeiro, RJ, Brazil. The page also describes the club as next to A.B.B.R.
- **Competition:** The **Festival de Bandas do Clube Carioca**. The page advertises an elimination round intended to select a band for the opportunity to record a **live concert CD**.
- **Outcome:** **The band did not advance beyond the eliminations and was not selected for the live-CD recording opportunity**, according to the founder's later statement. The archived page was an event invitation; it does **not** report or independently confirm the result.
- **Estimated audience:** Approximately **200 people**, based on the founder's retrospective estimate, not audited admissions data. No claim is made about the attendees' associations with the channel.
- **Public outreach:** The page invites readers to support the group and thanks friends and family who attended the earlier October 15 appearance. Neither statement identifies members of the audience.
- **Entry arrangements:** The historical announcement discusses payment/reservation by bank deposit, confirmation by email and inclusion of names at the door. This is evidence of a **web-to-venue coordination mechanism**, but the contact and payment identifiers are deliberately omitted.
- **Organizer:** The historical page identifies an organizer by the first name **Carlos** and provides a phone number that is not transcribed. His relationship, if any, with the Canal Barra organization has not been established.
- **Founder-reported background:** The band's activities resumed after BarMan returned from Los Angeles. That detail comes from retrospective testimony, not the archived page itself.

## Historical value and limits

The **2002 Canal Barra Fifth Anniversary Party at Ilha dos Pescadores** is a **different event**, at a different location with different supporting records and setlist. Each of the two October 2000 Sundays appears at the **same level in the historical chronology** as that anniversary; this does not imply identical scale, turnout or historical importance.

Taken together, the records establish the existence of a period **Canal Barra-branded musical announcement**, a contemporaneous reference to the first performance, a planned second performance, publicity for original compositions, a physical venue and web-coordinated invitation arrangements. They do **not** demonstrate quantified trust, online-to-offline conversion rates, safety outcomes, or verified attendance.

**Outstanding checks:** festival flyers, official competition records, preserved original HTML, the initial online publication date, ticket records, independently corroborated outcomes, photographs and testimony about the songs actually played.
